# Game experience and design standard

Updated: 25 August 2026

## Product feeling

Rakhi Battle is a game that plays like an interactive short video, not a website wrapped around a quiz. The experience should create anticipation, quick decisions, scene changes and a satisfying reveal.

## Runtime rules

- Playable flow uses one fixed `100dvh` shell.
- No document scrolling during lobby, questions, waiting, result, paywall, paid reveal or poster scenes.
- One primary action and one clear visual idea per scene.
- Long results become tap-through cards, never a report page that requires scrolling.
- Respect safe areas, browser bars, keyboard opening and short phone heights.
- Reduced-motion users receive the same information without forced animation.

## Visual hierarchy

- Opening scene must show the game name, emotional hook and start action without scrolling.
- Question is the dominant text; phone target weight is approximately 750–800.
- Answer labels use approximately 650–700 with large full-row tap targets.
- Progress and navigation remain visible without overlapping each other.
- Buttons must look tappable through contrast, depth, pressed feedback and short transitions.
- Both siblings should remain visible in key artwork; do not crop the sister or brother into a secondary role.
- Festive cues support the action and cannot make the screen look like a static poster.

## Motion and interaction

- Use a short cinematic opening, staged text arrival and scene-to-scene transitions.
- Choices receive immediate press/selection feedback.
- Result reveal should sequence score, type, teasers, offer and free continuation.
- Animation must be purposeful, fast and interruptible. Avoid long intros that delay play.
- Sound controls may be added later, but visual comprehension cannot depend on audio.

## Result and selling sequence

1. Show Memory Sync and the research-inspired relationship type.
2. Show two animated MATCH/OBJECTION memories.
3. Quantify locked curiosity truthfully, for example: “You disagreed on 3 memories.”
4. Offer ₹49 Exact Answer Reveal.
5. Preview the ₹99 Rakhi Gift Pack with a blurred premium asset.
6. “Continue free” goes directly to the simple share card.
7. Paid content is delivered card by card within the same game shell.

The free share card is necessary for distribution. Premium poster/certificate quality belongs to the Gift Pack.

## Psychology and tone

- Shared memory agreement is not relationship quality.
- Every score receives an affectionate result.
- Use Warmth, Conflict, Rivalry and Balance as research-inspired entertainment dimensions.
- Never diagnose, shame, rank love or imply a low match means a weak bond.
- Either participant may pay or share the optional gift hint.

## Mobile acceptance checks

Verify at minimum:

- 360×800 and 390×844 viewports;
- Android Chrome and iPhone Safari;
- Instagram/WhatsApp in-app browsers where available;
- no unintended vertical or horizontal scroll;
- CTA, back control and progress never overlap;
- all text remains readable with system font substitution;
- keyboard does not hide required controls;
- reload/back restores the correct scene;
- poster preview, download and native share work.

A desktop review cannot substitute for these checks.
