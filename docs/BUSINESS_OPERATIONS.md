# Business, payments and launch operations

Updated: 25 August 2026

This file records the operating decision, not legal or tax advice. Reconfirm gateway, GST, income-tax, FEMA and Polish-residency consequences with the relevant provider or adviser before material scale.

## Operating owner

The launch will be genuinely operated by the user's wife as an India-based individual/sole proprietor.

This requires substance, not name-lending:

- Razorpay KYC and settlement account are hers.
- Website seller identity matches the merchant record.
- She controls receipts, refunds, support and commercial decisions.
- Revenue and expenses are recorded in her books and tax filings.
- Any capital, IP use, gift or loan from the user should be documented appropriately.
- Do not route the user's own business through her merely to avoid NRI, Polish or tax rules.

No PAN, Aadhaar, bank details or KYC files belong in this repository.

## Razorpay launch requirements

Prepare:

- matching owner name, PAN/Aadhaar, phone/email and Indian settlement account;
- accurate activity description: online entertainment with personalised digital reports/cards;
- live site and visible Pricing, Contact, Terms, Privacy, Refund and Digital Delivery pages;
- monitored support identity;
- Razorpay live approval and UPI/card checkout;
- Worker secrets stored outside Git;
- one controlled real purchase, failure, retry, reload-unlock, delivery and refund test.

Keep `PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false` until all conditions pass. Do not use a personal UPI QR as the normal flow because it breaks automatic verification, entitlement and refund operations.

## Meta and support setup

Required before paid promotion:

- dedicated Facebook Page;
- dedicated Instagram professional profile;
- both connected in Meta Business Suite;
- ad account and billing method;
- wife as owner/admin and user as an authorised administrator if desired;
- WhatsApp Business or another monitored customer-support route;
- attribution and conversion events tested from ad preview to verified purchase.

AdSense and Google Ads are not part of the initial launch.

## Budget gates

Total family-money ceiling: ₹15,000.

| Stage | Maximum new spend | Proceed only when |
|---|---:|---|
| Accounts, CA/support and infrastructure | ₹5,000 | Seller/payment structure is clear and site is reliable |
| First traffic test | ₹900–₹1,000 | Real checkout, unlock and analytics pass |
| Second test | up to ₹2,000 | Creator completion and sibling link-open behaviour justify continuation |
| Controlled scale | remaining ceiling | Buyer conversion, revenue per acquired visitor and viral coefficient improve |

Stop immediately if payment delivery, refunds, site reliability or attribution fail. Scale primarily from earned revenue, not additional family money.

Working targets:

- validation: first genuine purchases and measurable downstream sharing;
- operating target: ₹25,000–₹50,000 gross;
- stretch: ₹1 lakh;
- viral breakout: ₹5 lakh+;
- ₹50 lakh is not a planning assumption.

## KPI gates

The main economic KPI is new child battles per completed pair. Supporting KPIs:

- creator completion;
- challenge open from share;
- sibling completion;
- completed pairs;
- checkout starts;
- verified buyer conversion;
- average order value;
- revenue per acquired visitor;
- viral coefficient and transfer time;
- refund/support failure rate.

Do not scale merely because clicks or views are cheap.

## Tax and compliance boundary

- Income tax applies to profit under the genuine owner's circumstances.
- GST registration and product classification must be reviewed before approaching the applicable turnover threshold or any compulsory-registration condition.
- Maintain gateway settlement reports, invoices, refunds, ad/creator bills, hosting/domain bills, bank statements and an income/expense ledger.
- The user's NRE/NRO/FEMA and Polish tax or immigration position remains relevant if he later owns, controls or operates the business from Poland.
- Bank destination does not by itself determine who earned the income or where it must be reported.
