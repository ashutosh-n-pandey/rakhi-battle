# Rakhi Battle psychology and revenue model

## Product boundary

Rakhi Battle is festive entertainment, not a diagnostic instrument. The result may say
"research-inspired" because its dimensions follow sibling-relationship research, but it
must never claim to clinically measure, diagnose or prove a relationship.

## Research basis

- Furman and Buhrmester (1985) found four underlying sibling-relationship factors:
  warmth/closeness, conflict, rivalry and relative status/power.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3987418/
- Stocker, Lanthier and Furman (1997) reported three independent adult-sibling
  dimensions: warmth, conflict and rivalry.
  https://doi.org/10.1037/0893-3200.11.2.210
- Buist and Vermande (2014) identified harmonious (high warmth/low conflict),
  affect-intense (high warmth/high conflict) and conflictual (low warmth/high conflict)
  clusters.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24866727/

The game uses four visible dimensions: Warmth, Conflict, Rivalry and Balance. Memory
Sync is separate: agreement measures whether two accounts align, not whether the bond
is good or bad.

## Public archetypes

| Research pattern | Rakhi Battle title | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harmonious | Safe-Harbour Siblings | warmth >= 60, conflict < 50 |
| Affect-intense | Firecracker Family | warmth >= 60, conflict >= 50 |
| Conflict-forward | Courtroom Companions | warmth < 60, conflict >= 50 |
| Low-involved | Independent Allies | warmth < 60, conflict < 50 |

All descriptions remain affectionate. Rivalry and balance appear as independent profile
bars and must not be treated as moral scores.

## Revenue ladder

- Free: complete game, Memory Sync, relationship archetype, two MATCH/OBJECTION
  teasers and a simple share card.
- ₹49 Exact Answer Reveal: both answers for all eight questions, four-dimension profile
  and private playful verdict.
- ₹99 Rakhi Gift Pack: everything in Reveal plus premium festive poster and sibling
  certificate. An existing ₹49 purchase upgrades for ₹50.
- Either participant may pay. One purchase unlocks the same battle for both.
- "Ask my sibling to gift this unlock" is an optional sharing action. Never require a
  particular gender to pay.

## Viral proof rules

Only database-derived counters may be shown:

- completed sibling stories;
- participants active in the preceding 10 minutes;
- family chains containing more than one completed battle;
- highest mutually opted-in Hall of Fame score.

Hide zero-value counters. Never seed, round up or fabricate activity.
